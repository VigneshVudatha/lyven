1. What is LYVEN? (In Plain English)

LYVEN is a modern online shopping store for an Indian streetwear clothing brand. It sells heavyweight graphic-print oversized T-shirts made of 240 GSM Combed French Terry Cotton (a thick, premium fabric that holds its boxy shape).

2. How Every Page on the Website Works

1. Homepage (/)

The main storefront. It features a bold dark design, displays the clothing collection, tells the brand's story, and shows small popup alerts at the bottom of the screen when other customers purchase items.

2. Shop Page (/shop)

Allows customers to browse the full catalogue, filter by style (Graphic Tees, Minimalist, Oversized Drops), and sort shirts by price or newest releases.

3. Product Details Page (/products/[slug])

Shows high-resolution Front and Back views of each T-shirt, an interactive size selector (S, M, L, XL, XXL), live stock counters, fabric details, washing tips, and an Add to Bag button.

4. Persistent Shopping Cart (/cart & Slide-Out Drawer)

Customers can view their selected items, change quantities, and see if they qualify for free shipping. If a customer refreshes the page or leaves the website, their cart items are automatically saved.

5. Indian Checkout Page (/checkout)

A fast checkout built specifically for India. It automatically verifies that phone numbers are real 10 digits starting with 6, 7, 8, or 9, checks that PIN codes are 6 digits, and lets customers choose their State.

6. Online Payment & Cash on Delivery (COD)

Customers can pay online using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, UPI, Cards, and NetBanking through Razorpay, or select Cash on Delivery to pay cash when their package arrives at their door.

7. Order Tracking Portal (/track)

Customers can enter their Order Reference number (e.g. LYV-20261021-9988A) to track their order status: Confirmed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered, with direct links to BlueDart or Delhivery.

8. Customer Accounts (/account & /my-orders)

Customers can register with their email address and password to view all their past orders and download official tax invoices.

9. Admin Control Panel (/admin)

A password-protected management area for the store owner. The owner can see total revenue, view all customer orders, enter courier tracking numbers, and update inventory counts for any size with one click.

10. Custom T-Shirt Studio (/custom-design)

Customers can upload their own logo or artwork (PNG or JPG up to 10MB), pick a shirt color, choose print placement (Front, Back, Pocket), and transfer the design directly into WhatsApp to chat with the store owner.

11. WhatsApp Concierge Float

A green WhatsApp button in the corner of every page allows customers to start a chat with customer support (+91 83282 21844) instantly.

12. GST Tax Invoices

Automatically calculates official 5% Indian apparel GST (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST for Karnataka, or 5% IGST for other states) under HSN Code 6109, with a one-click Print button.

3. How the Website Works Behind the Scenes

  • Fast & Responsive: Built with Next.js 16 and React 19 so it opens instantly on smartphones and computers.
  • Cloud Database with Zero-Crash Fallback: Connected to Supabase PostgreSQL to save orders and products. If the database is disconnected, the website has an in-memory fallback so it never crashes during demos or testing.
  • Tamper-Proof Pricing: The server recalculates all prices itself. Customers cannot alter prices in their browser to buy cheap shirts.
  • Overselling Prevention: When customers order, stock is deducted atomically so two people cannot buy the same last shirt simultaneously.

5. Simple 3-Step Guide to Put Your Website Live

  1. Create Supabase Database: Create a project at supabase.com, run the SQL script in supabase/migrations/, and copy your database keys into .env.local.
  2. Add Razorpay Keys: Copy your Razorpay Key ID and Secret from dashboard.razorpay.com into .env.local to accept online payments.
  3. Deploy to Vercel: Connect your GitHub repository to vercel.com and click Deploy. Your store is now live!
